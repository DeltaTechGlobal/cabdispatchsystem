How it works

Each phone line gets one extra SIP extension, which you create with your phone provider and add to the line’s ring group. CDS rings along with your phones but never answers, so your calls and call quality stay exactly as they are.

  • Up to 6 lines per office
  • Give each line a business name, such as “Airport Cars”
  • Each line shows whether it’s connected, in Settings
  • Send a test call to check the pop-up before going live

Tested with

Soho66, Onsim and Virgin Media O2 Business. Most SIP providers that let you add an extension should work.

No desk phones to change

Keep your handsets, numbers and provider. Nothing is installed on your computers.

Not supported

Microsoft Teams Phone doesn’t work with CDS caller ID.
