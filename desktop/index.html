CDS on your computer

Open CDS from its own icon on the desktop, taskbar or Dock, like any other program. It takes one click from inside CDS, or a small download.

Using Chrome or Edge? You don’t need a download. Sign in to CDS, open Settings › Preferences and click Install CDS. CDS then opens in its own window, without the browser’s tabs.

Windows

For Windows 10 and 11, with any browser. Adds CDS Portal to the desktop and the Start menu. It opens CDS in your usual browser. No administrator is needed.

  1. Download CDS-Portal-Setup.exe and open it.
  2. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Click Finish. CDS opens, and the icon is on your desktop.
  4. To keep it on the taskbar, open Start, right-click CDS Portal and choose Pin to taskbar.

To remove it, go to Settings › Apps, find CDS Portal and choose Uninstall.

Download for Windows

Mac

For macOS 12 or later, on Apple silicon and Intel Macs. Adds CDS Portal to Applications and Launchpad. It opens CDS in your usual browser.

  1. Download CDS-Portal.dmg and open it.
  2. Drag CDS Portal onto the Applications folder.
  3. Open Applications and drag CDS Portal to the Dock to keep it one click away.

On macOS Sonoma or later, Safari can do this without a download: open CDS, then choose File › Add to Dock. To remove the app, drag it from Applications to the Bin.
