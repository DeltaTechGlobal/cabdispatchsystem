Fixed fares and mileage rates, priced your way

CDS prices every trip from your own tariff. Set fixed fares between your airports, stations and postcodes, fill a whole grid of prices at once, and let mileage rates cover everything else.

Real CDS screen with sample data.

Minicab fixed fares by postcode

A fixed fare runs between two ends, each a place or a postcode. The postcode can be as broad or as exact as you like, and the most specific match wins.

  • A full postcode (N1 9AL), a sector (N1 9), a district (N1) or a whole area (N)
  • Both ways by default, or one way only
  • Bigger vehicles get their own price, or a % or £ uplift on the standard fare
  • Trips with stops, and anywhere without a fixed fare, are priced by mileage
  • N1 ⇄ London Stansted Airport£75.00
  • N1 9 ⇄ London Heathrow Airport£59.90
  • EC1 ⇄ London Luton Airport£70.00
  • N ⇄ London City Airport£45.00

Sample prices.

Price a whole grid at once

Pick up to eight of your places as columns, and postcode districts near a point or in chosen areas as rows. Type, paste from a spreadsheet, or select cells and set a price, add pounds or add a percentage.

  • Districts within 0.5 to 20 miles of a postcode or place
  • Or whole postcode areas, such as SW or W
  • Save hundreds of routes in one go

Airports, stations and the Underground, ready to add

Add places from the CDS catalogue instead of typing coordinates. An airport brings its terminals with it.

  • UK airports and terminals
  • National Rail stations
  • 270 London Underground and 45 DLR stations
  • Ready-made sets such as “London airports” and “Central London (Zone 1)”
  • Your own places too, like a hotel or a hospital

Taxi fare software that knows airports

Airport runs come with their own costs. CDS keeps them separate from the fare.

Mileage rates

A rate per mile and a minimum fare for each vehicle type, with an example fare as you edit.

Drop-off and parking

A drop-off charge per airport, and “plus parking” when the driver waits. Passed on at cost, with no commission taken.

Account prices

Give a business customer a discount or uplift, or their own negotiated rates per vehicle type.
