A taxi booking website in your name

Your page lives at book.cabdispatchsystem.com with your office code. Customers see your name, not ours.

  • Your logo, name and brand colour
  • Your phone number, for customers who’d rather call
  • Links to your own terms and privacy policy
  • A page for each of your business names

Add it to your site in minutes

Copy one of three snippets from Settings and give it to whoever looks after your website.

  • A floating “Book a taxi” button on every page
  • The booking form inside one of your pages
  • A plain link for a button, a menu or a social profile
  • A QR code for cards, flyers and the cab windows

Online booking on your terms

You decide what customers can book and when. The address search offers your own places, so your fixed fares apply online just as they do on the phone.

  • Allow as-soon-as-possible bookings, or only bookings ahead with a notice period
  • Choose which vehicle types customers can book
  • Payment to the driver by cash or card
  • Let customers cancel until a driver is assigned, or until the driver sets off
  • Limit open bookings per customer and sign-in texts per day

Safe for you, simple for customers

Sign in with a texted code

Customers confirm their UK mobile with a six-digit code. No passwords to forget, and limits stop people running up your text bill.

A trip page for every booking

Customers follow their trip, see their driver and car once one is on the way, and cancel within your rules.

Straight onto your board

Online bookings arrive in Upcoming jobs marked Web, with the same texts as any other booking.

Online card payment isn’t available yet: customers pay the driver by cash or card.
