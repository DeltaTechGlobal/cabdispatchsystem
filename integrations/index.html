Works with the services your office already uses

CDS connects to partner networks, text and email providers, phone lines, maps and your website. You keep the accounts and pay the providers directly, and there’s no hardware to buy.

Book a demo Watch the demo

Partners and bookings

Work from partner companies and from your own website comes straight onto the board.

GNet partner network

Take trips from chauffeur and private hire partners through GNet. Status, driver, car and final charges go back automatically. GNet partner trips

Your website

A booking page in your name, or a booking button your web designer adds with one line of code. Online booking

CDS Driver app

Jobs, offers and live location from drivers’ Android and iPhone phones, and texts for drivers who aren’t on the app. Driver app

Texts, email, phones and maps

Connected in Settings, mostly in a 30-minute screen-share with us when you start.

Text messages

SMS Gateway for Android (a free app on a spare Android phone), SMS8, The SMS Works or Twilio. Customers’ replies come back to your inbox. Texts and emails

Email

Trip emails from your own address through Gmail, Microsoft 365, Brevo, Amazon SES or any SMTP account. Replies come back to you.

Phone lines

Caller ID through one extra SIP extension per line, tested with Soho66, Onsim and Virgin Media O2 Business. Up to 6 lines. Caller ID

Google Maps

Routes with live traffic and alternative routes. Your office can use its own Google key, so map billing is in your name.

TfL PCO returns

The weekly driver and vehicle CSV files TfL asks London operators for, ready to upload. PCO returns

Any computer

CDS runs in a modern browser on Windows, Mac or a tablet. Nothing to install, and updates arrive on their own.

Connect your own software

Corporate booking tools, hotel and concierge systems, and other dispatch systems that want to send you work. We’re working with partners on a booking API that sends bookings into CDS and live trip status back.

Tell us what you’d connect
  • Bookings inYour partner’s system sends the booking. CDS prices it from your fares and puts it on the board.
  • Status backDriver assigned, on the way, at the pickup, on board and completed, as they happen.
  • Your rulesEach partner books to its own account, at its own rates, invoiced with the rest.

Don’t see your provider?

Tell us which SMS, email or phone provider you use, or which partners send you work, and we’ll tell you how it connects.
